# Asset provenance ledger

Every visual and audio asset the site ships must appear here with its origin and license.

**Rules:**
- No AI-generated images, textures, photographs, audio or 3D models.
- Only original work, or files with a clear license that allows redistribution.
- Personal files (photos, the resume PDF) are stripped of metadata before they are added.

## Seamless studio environment

`StudioEnvironment.ts` builds one original indexed floor/backdrop: 2,560 triangles and 1,365 vertices, one material/draw call with no image maps or per-frame geometry work. The continuous backdrop still spans 160 m across, extends 28 m forward and rises 24 m. A small 12 mm floor fillet fits beneath an original 84 × 14 mm matte baseboard (12 triangles, one extra draw call), rather than intersecting a large floor curve. Its endpoints remain outside supported views. The requested coordinated palette is wall `#A8BDB0`, floor `#555957`, baseboard `#E5E3DC` and side atmosphere `#899D94`; broad surface variation remains below 1.3%, with no screen grain or fog.

The floor remains y = -743 mm and the display plane z = -630 mm, approximately 75 mm behind the desktop. The current desk, chair, scattered cubes and tabletop objects retain their transforms. All current poster images, paper dimensions, authored layout, spacing, proportions, putty mounts and print-depth fix remain intact; the prints sit within 3 mm of the wall.

Free Camera retains its zoom range, floor clearance, target and entry/exit behavior. Its yaw is bounded by the viewport's horizontal field of view with a 10-degree margin (about ±51 degrees at 16:9, ±44 degrees at 21:9 and up to ±65 degrees in portrait). Bounds update on resize. This prevents wide views from looking past the open sweep; there are no added side walls to hide those angles.

The old wall/floor occlusion band remains removed; the current ivory baseboard is original simple code geometry. Existing object contact footprints now read their actual alpha falloff, with lighter opacity; floor footprints fade fully before the cove instead of being cut off by its raised geometry. The key light keeps soft real-time shadows, supported by neutral bounce/fill and the original reflection environment.

Verification: `node tools/model-pipeline/check-studio.js` checks geometry budget, smooth normals, tint range, desk clearance, poster mounting, contact alpha/floor clipping and 14,625 frustum rays across intro, desk, drawer, idle and orbit extremes at five viewport ratios. Run alongside the camera, drawer, input, audio and intro checks, TypeScript and production build. No source model, baked texture or poster image is modified by this environment change. The prior studio revision was checked in the browser. For this clearance/controller-removal revision, fresh front/side/Free Camera screenshots could not be captured: the in-app browser repeatedly timed out attaching a localhost tab (visible and background modes). Automated coverage, collision, mount-depth and drawer interaction checks passed; fresh visual review remains required.

## Production delivery packaging (2026-11-09)

Editable GLBs, transforms, materials, poster files and scene layout are retained. Six `.meshopt.glb` delivery variants are generated by `tools/loading/package-models.cjs`: headphones, mouse, chair, laptop, Porsche and speakers. Existing asset authors, licences and attribution above continue to apply to these delivery variants.

Modifications are lossless geometry buffer encoding (`EXT_meshopt_compression`, no extra quantization, reordering or decimation) and lossless PNG-to-WebP encoding (`EXT_texture_webp`, no resizing). The offline pipeline asserts byte-identical decoded buffer views using the exact decoder bundled with the installed Three.js, and identical RGBA pixels for each converted image. Original JPEGs are copied unchanged. Source/delivery hashes and exact sizes are recorded in `tools/loading/model-sizes.json`. Raw versions of these six models are excluded from the production copy, avoiding duplicate downloads/deployment files.

Meshoptimizer encoder 0.22.0 is MIT-licensed, an offline dependency under `tools/loading/` only. The runtime decoder is the MIT-licensed implementation already distributed with Three.js 0.137.5 (`three/examples/jsm/libs/meshopt_decoder.module.js`). No new runtime dependencies, textures, artwork, generated visuals or third-party models are introduced. See `tools/loading/README.md` for reproduction.

The decoder copyright and complete MIT permission notice are shipped at `static/licenses/meshoptimizer.txt` (production URL `licenses/meshoptimizer.txt`), since the minifier does not retain its original line comments.
